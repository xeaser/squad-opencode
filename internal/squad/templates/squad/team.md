## How to work

1. Talk to the **squad** primary agent first for multi-role work.
2. Confirm cast with a clear **yes** when the coordinator proposes the team.
3. Invoke specialists with `@lead`, `@frontend`, `@backend`, `@tester` when you know the owner.
4. Humans approve merges, product calls, and security-sensitive changes.
5. Durable decisions go in `.squad/decisions.md`.
